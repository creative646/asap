Supplement Facts Label Builder

Fill in the formula on the left; the label on the right updates live in the standard FDA layout. Line weights, type sizes, row pitch and indents follow 21 CFR 101.36(e) and match FDA's high-resolution Supplement Facts examples. Export a print-ready PDF or PNG at true physical size.

Serving information

Allowed variants: "Amount Per Serving", "Amount Per Capsule", "Amount Per Tablet", "Amount Per 2 Tablets", etc.

Dietary ingredients

With %DV — (b)(2): nutrients that have a Daily Value (vitamins, minerals, calories, fat, carbs…). List them first, in FDA order.
No DV — (b)(3): other dietary ingredients (herbs, amino acids, blends, source compounds). They get the "†" symbol and are separated from (b)(2) by a heavy bar.

Footnotes

Required footnotes are generated automatically from the symbols used on the label ("* Percent Daily Values are based on a 2,000 calorie diet.", "† Daily Value not established.").

Ingredient statement (below the panel)

Placed immediately below the Supplement Facts box, outside it (21 CFR 101.4(g)). Use "Ingredients:" when the source compounds are not named in the panel.

Size & output

*True size on screen depends on your monitor's DPI; the PDF and PNG are always exact.
